I am a Ph.D. candidate in Library and Information Science at Yonsei University, where I am a member of the SNIS Lab. Alongside my doctoral studies, I work as a medical librarian, managing library operations and supporting medical research.

My research examines the social dimensions of information and knowledge practices in academic settings, with a particular focus on social capital, academic library spaces, and the evolving relationship between human interaction and AI.
